# Agentic Education Practitioners Association at Agentic-ED.org

The Agentic Education Practitioners Association consists of multiple guilds. Our guilds build and freely share **open-source AI agents and agent skills under the MIT License**. We also host educational programs where practitioners can share what they learn from both building skills and agents and using them in their respeective practices.

[Explore the guilds](https://agentic-ed.org/guilds) · [Browse the repositories](https://github.com/orgs/Agentic-ED/repositories) · [Sign up](https://agentic-ed.org/signup)

## Find an asset you can use

We recommend that you start with the work you want to do. An **agent skill** gives an AI environment instructions for a particular task and may include scripts, examples, or reference files. An **agent** uses instructions and tools to carry out a workflow. Download the asset from its repository and check its README for setup, dependencies, and compatibility with your environment, such as Claude, Gemini, or Codex.


## Find your Guild

Each Guild focuses on one area of practice and takes its name from a group of animals. The animal is also the guild’s AI steward. Members choose priorities, the steward helps review contributions, and Human leaders of the Guild approve releases. That's a high level take on it, but there are mechanics associated with AI budgets, membership voting, and project prioritizations.

## Learn and contribute

Bring your own AI environment to build or improve an asset. A useful contribution can be small: an example input that exposes a limitation, a clearer instruction, or a test that shows whether a skill does what it claims.

1. Choose the Guild closest to the work you do or aspire to do. Read its repository instructions and check open issues for related efforts.
2. Open an issue to describe the task or propose an agent or skill. If you already have a change ready, follow the repository’s pull request process.
3. Include a sample input, the outcome you expected, what actually happened, and the AI environment you tested. Use examples you have permission to share publicly.
